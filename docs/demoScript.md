## Presenter Technical Guidelines

1. **Pacing:** Maintain a deliberate, crisp delivery rate of approximately 130 words per minute to complete comfortably within the 180-second window.
2. **Blockchain Focus:** Emphasize the core blockchain value propositions: separation of duties (`VerifierCannotBeRelayer`), mathematical mass balance, non-transferability, and public verifiability without platform lock-in.
3. **Clarity on Environmental Metrics:** State clearly that work credits account for verified wet biomass kilograms, leaving certified carbon accounting to formal registry methodologies.
