## Presenter Guidelines

1. **Pacing:** Keep spoken delivery crisp and steady (~130 words per minute).
2. **Clarity on Blockchain Value:** Emphasize why blockchain matters here: mass balance enforcement, separation of duties, non-transferability, and public verifiability without platform lock-in.
3. **Honesty:** Do not refer to work credits as carbon credits; explicitly describe them as verified biomass accounting tokens.
